# Using the app

A tour of every page. The sidebar has them all, in groups: *Today* (the home page),
*Your applications* (Roles, Applications, Interviews, Offers), *People and companies*, *Files
and notes* and *Review* (Insights, Timeline). Along
the top, on every page:

- the name, which takes you back to [Today](#today-home);
- **New application**;
- the search box (press <kbd>/</kbd>), which finds anything;
- **?** (*How it works*), the everyday loop in five numbered steps;
- the [language](#language) switch;
- light/dark mode.

## The basics: companies, roles and applications

- A **company** is somewhere you might work.
- A **role** is a job at a company (title, location, work mode, salary range or day rate
  and IR35). It can exist before you apply: see [Roles to decide](#roles-to-decide).
- An **application** is you going for a role, by one **route**: directly, through an
  agency or recruiter, or by referral. Most of the app is about applications.
- An **agency** is a recruitment agency. A **person** is anyone you deal with: an agency
  recruiter, an in-house recruiter or head of talent, a hiring manager, an interviewer, a
  referrer. Each has a page with as many emails, phone numbers and links as you like.

Create an application with **New application** at the top of any page: pick or type the
company and role, and how it came about. You don't need to add the company, agency or
recruiter first:

- **Directly**: you applied, or someone at the company reached out. Put them in *Who reached
  out?* (an in-house recruiter or head of talent) and they're added as a person at the
  company.
- **Through a recruiter**: an agency and/or a recruiter there.
- **Referral**: link the person who referred you from the application's People card.

Companies, roles, agencies and people typed in for the first time are created for you.

## Roles to decide

After a call with a recruiter you often have a few roles to think about before applying for
any. Add them as **roles** first, then decide on each:

- **Add role**: on the **Roles** page, on a company's or person's page, or (best) **Add roles
  from this call** under a call that happened on the recruiter's page. A role has a company
  (type a new one to add it), a title, who told you about it and on which call, a job ad
  link, location, work mode, pay (salary range, or day rate and IR35 for contracts) and notes.
  **Job description or other files** attaches files as you add it (a PDF of the job spec).
  **Add and add another** keeps the person and call for the next one.
- **Apply** makes the application and opens it: through the recruiter's agency if they're at
  one, otherwise directly with them as your contact. Pick the stage (Applied by default) and
  the date.
- **Interested** does the same at your starting stage (*Interested* by default) with no
  applied date: you want it but haven't applied yet. The role then shows as *Interested* until
  its application gets to *Applied*, or is closed before then (the role then says how it
  ended, e.g. *Withdrawn*). Move it on from the application; moving it to *Applied* or later
  fills in *Applied on* if it's empty. The button only shows when your
  workflow starts before *Applied*. In either dialog, the applied date follows the stage you
  pick (empty before *Applied*, today from *Applied* on) until you change the date yourself.
- **Pass** keeps the role with your reason ("rate too low"), under *Passed*. **Reconsider**
  puts it back. The pencil edits a role; the bin deletes it (not once it has an application).
  Change the person on a role and its open applications that had the old person (or nobody)
  take the new one where they fit how you applied: an agency application if they're at its
  agency, a direct one if they work at the role's company. Each says so on its timeline
  ("Recruiter changed from … to …, on the role"). How you applied never changes: one where the
  new person doesn't fit keeps the old one, with a note saying the role changed hands. Closed,
  archived and referral applications, and ones you'd given someone else, are left alone, and
  taking a role's person away changes no application. Changing an application's recruiter
  yourself is noted on its timeline too. These notes don't count as activity, so they don't
  reset how long an application has been quiet.
- Deleting a call keeps the roles from it; they just aren't linked to a call any more.

**A role's page.** Click a role's name anywhere (the Roles page, a company, a person, a call,
Today, an application's *Role* card, search, the Timeline) to open its page:

- what it is (pay, terms, location, the job ad), who told you about it and on which call,
  when you added it, and why you passed if you did; your notes on it;
- **Apply**, **Interested**, **Pass** or **Reconsider**, **Edit** and **Delete**;
- the application you made from it, with its stage;
- **To-dos**, **Notes**, **Links** and **Files** about the role. Drop the job description on
  **Files**: once you apply, it shows on the application's **Files** card too, under *From
  the role* (it's the same file, not a copy). A paperclip and count next to a role's name
  means it has files.

The **Roles** page shows roles *To decide* (the default), *Interested* (an application that
hasn't got to *Applied* yet), *Applied* (one that got to *Applied* at some point, even if it
was closed later), *Closed* (every application closed before any got to *Applied*: withdrawn, rejected or
ghosted while only interested; the badge says how the latest one ended, e.g. *Withdrawn*, and
the role links to that one), *Passed* or *All*. An application you record straight at a closed
stage with an applied date (a past one that was rejected) counts as applied. Roles
to decide also show under *Your move* on Today. On
the Timeline you'll see who mentioned which role, and the ones you passed on. A role you
applied for through **New application** with the same title at the same company counts as
applied too.

## Today (home)

Everything that needs you, in one place, sorted by what you do about it. At the top, one line:
active applications, interviews in the next two weeks and offers to answer (each opens its
page), with the pipeline of active applications by stage underneath. Then three groups; on a
wide screen *Your move* is on the left and the other two sit beside it:

**Your move**: things to do now, in this order:

- **Replies to read**: applications and people who've replied that you still need to read
  (see [Waiting to hear back](#waiting-to-hear-back)), oldest first; **Read it** clears it.
- **Offers to answer**: pending offers with a reply due in the next two weeks, or overdue.
- **How did it go?**: interview rounds and calls whose time has passed; mark them *Done* (a
  call: *Happened*) or *Didn't happen*. Each asks first: a round's *Done* for its debrief and
  the questions asked, *Didn't happen* for what happened (saved on the round, or in the call's
  notes). Nothing changes until you save; the notes can stay empty.
- **To-dos**: your own reminders due today or earlier, or with no date (see [To-dos](#to-dos)).
  Add one here, about anything or nothing in particular.
- **Follow up**: applications whose follow-up date is today or earlier.
- **Roles to decide**: roles you've added with no application that you haven't passed on
  (see [Roles to decide](#roles-to-decide)), oldest first, with **Apply**, **Interested** and
  **Pass**.

**Coming up**: nothing to do yet. Interviews, task deadlines, [calls](#calls-and-meetings) and
to-dos with a later date, soonest first, then rounds not booked yet.

**Waiting on them**: who you're waiting for.

- **Waiting to hear back**: applications and people you've replied to (see
  [Waiting to hear back](#waiting-to-hear-back)), longest first. It turns red once you've
  waited as long as the stage allows; **Heard back** clears it, and **They've replied** moves
  it to *Replies to read*.
- **Gone quiet**: active applications with no activity for as long as their stage allows
  or more (7 days in Applied, 5 in Screen and Interviewing, and so on; see
  [Customising](customising.md)).

Each item is a tile. On most of them (replies, offers, calls, interviews, follow-ups and
anything you're waiting on) the buttons stay faint until you point at the tile or tab into it;
on a touch screen they always show. On an application: set a follow-up date (tomorrow, in 3 days, next week), **snooze** it
(3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks) so it stops nagging, or mark it **Ghosted**. A snoozed application,
one with a follow-up date in the future, or one with an interview booked ahead never counts as
gone quiet. Click an application's name for its quick view.

At the bottom, folded away: **Recent activity** (the last two weeks of the
[Timeline](#timeline), leaving out companies, agencies and people being added) and **How it
works** (the same numbered steps as the **?** button).

With nothing in the tracker yet, it shows a *Getting started* card instead, with a **New
application** button. Once you've added anything else (people, companies, agencies, calls
or roles) but no applications, Today shows it as usual under a short *No applications yet*
card that sums up what you have (recent activity then includes what you've added).

Today replaced the separate Overview and Next actions pages; old *Next actions* links and
bookmarks open it.

### To-dos

Short reminders in your own words, for anything the tracker doesn't already chase: *they
messaged me, I need to reply*, *look into the company to see if I like what they do*, *ask
for the salary band*.

- **Add one** from the *To-dos* card on an application, company, agency or person page; from
  the to-do button (a list with ticks) on a role, wherever roles are listed; or under *To-dos*
  on Today, where **About** picks what it's about (or leave it empty). A date is optional.
- **Tick it off** with its checkbox; **Undo** in the message that pops up brings it back. It
  leaves Today; on its page it stays ticked (the last three), and
  **Done recently** under Today's to-dos lists the last ten, so you can untick one.
- **Edit** (the pencil) changes the words or the date; the bin deletes it (with **Undo** too).
- Overdue to-dos are marked in red, today's in orange. Each one links to what it's about (a
  role's opens its company).
- Deleting what a to-do is about keeps the to-do, about nothing in particular. Search finds
  to-dos too.

## Timeline

Everything that happened across the whole search, newest first, with interviews, calls and
offer reply deadlines still ahead under *Coming up*. It's in the *Review* group, next to
Insights. It includes:

- calls and meetings with people (not ones that didn't happen);
- roles people pitched to you, and the ones you passed on (with your reason);
- everything on application timelines: stage moves, calls, emails, notes, interviews,
  offers, files and documents sent (an application added past *Interested* shows as one
  *Added at …* entry, not the step filled in before it);
- each interview at its own time;
- notes on people, companies, agencies and roles (a note on a role counts for its company and
  the person who pitched it);
- people you're waiting to hear back from, and people whose reply you still need to read;
- companies, agencies, people and roles being added (bookkeeping, hidden unless you ask for
  it; a role someone pitched shows as their pitch instead).

Archived applications are included.

- **Period**: the last 30 days, 3 months (the default) or year, or all time.
- **About**: one company, agency or person. An application's entries count for its company,
  its agency and the people on it.
- **Show**: tick the kinds of thing to show: stage moves, messages, calls and meetings,
  interviews, offers, roles pitched or passed, notes, documents sent, files, companies,
  agencies and people added, other. Everything but *Companies, agencies and people added* is
  ticked to start with;
  **Everything**, **Usual** and **None** set them in one go.
- **List** groups entries by day. **Lanes** draws rows against time: dots for what happened
  (hollow for what's ahead) and a dashed line for now. By default it's **one row per role**,
  so a role's whole story reads left to right: the call it came up on, the pitch, then its
  application's stage moves, interviews and offers, or passing on it. Switch to one row per
  **person** (their calls and everything they're part of), **company** or **agency**. Click a
  row's name to open it; hover a dot to see what it was, and click it to open its
  application (or the role, person, company or agency it's about).

The view, period and *About* are in the page's address, so a bookmark or **Back** keeps them:
`/timeline?view=lanes` opens straight into lanes, and the **Timeline** button on a person's,
company's or agency's page opens `/timeline?about=contact:<id>&period=all` (or `company:`,
`agency:`). Someone who's since been deleted or merged away is ignored, so the Timeline
shows everything rather than nothing.

## Applications: list and board

The **list** has search (company, role, recruiter) and filters: stage, route, agency,
*Needs chasing*, and *Archived* (shows only archived applications). Click a row for a quick
view; open the full page from there.

The **board** shows a column per stage. Drag a card to move it; the usual next stages are
highlighted while you drag. Closed stages (Rejected, Withdrawn…) are hidden unless you
switch on *Show closed stages*, but appear while you drag so you can drop a card on them. Cards show the current interview round.

**A column per interview round** (a switch above the board, remembered) splits *Interviewing*
into *No round yet*, then *Round 1*, *Round 2*… up to the furthest any application has got
(empty ones in between up to Round 6), so you can see how far each one is. A card's round is its
next booked round, or else the last one done (cancelled rounds don't count). Rounds go in order
and come from the interviews you add on the application, so the round columns don't take drops:
drop a card on *No round yet* to move it into Interviewing (one that already has rounds goes
straight to its round), and it moves along as you add rounds. Any active stage whose id in
`config.toml` says "interview" (`interviewing`, `onsite_interview`) splits the same way,
whatever its name.

Every stage change is timestamped on the application's timeline, and **Undo last move**
reverts a mistake. By default you can move between any stages; you can make them strict
instead ([Customising](customising.md#strict-jira-style-transitions)).

**Every application starts at Interested** (your workflow's first stage). Add one straight at
*Applied* (or later, or closed) and its timeline says *Added as Interested*, then
*Interested → Applied* a second later, so Insights shows every application flowing from
*Interested*. That step was filled in for you: it doesn't count as time spent at *Interested*,
as an update from a recruiter or as a reply, and there's nothing to undo. On the
[Timeline](#timeline) page the pair is one entry, *Added at Applied*. Applications you added
before this came in got the same step when you upgraded (with the `initial` stage in your
`config.toml` at the time; ones at a stage you've since removed were left as they were).

## An application's page

Everything about one application:

- **Move to…**, **Undo last move**, and its stage, current round and tags.
- **Route**: how it came about (through a recruiter at an agency, directly, a referral), with
  the recruiter, agency or company linked to their page.
- **At a glance**: what this application needs, by the same rules as
  [Today](#today-home) (a reply to read, to-dos due by today or undated, an offer to
  answer in the next two weeks, a round waiting for an outcome, a follow-up due, gone quiet,
  waiting to hear back), each with how long it's been ("for 12 days", "overdue by 2 days");
  then what's coming up (booked rounds and ones not booked yet, later to-dos and follow-ups,
  an offer due later, a snooze); then its key dates as "3 weeks ago": added, applied, moved to
  its current stage, the last call, email or message, the last interview, and the last
  activity (things logged for a later date count once they happen). A closed or archived
  application has nothing to chase, though your own to-dos on it still show, as they do on
  Today. The quick view (clicking a row in the list or a card on the board) shows it
  too.
- **Details**: applied date, follow-up date, snooze, tags, and **Archived**.
- **Interviews**: numbered rounds in your own words ("Round 2 · Engineering manager chat"),
  with type, time or deadline, format and link, interviewers, prep, debrief, questions asked,
  and take-home briefs and repos; *Rescheduled* on one that moved (see [Interviews](#interviews)).
- **Documents sent**: which version of your CV and cover letter went with it.
- **Offer**: pay and terms, and revised offers (see [Offers](#offers)).
- **Notes**, **Links** (job ads, Google Docs, repos) and **Files** (PDFs, images, exported
  emails). **Files** also lists the role's files (the job description) under *From the role*;
  the *Role* card's title opens the [role's page](#roles-to-decide).
- **People**: who's involved, and as what (agency recruiter, internal recruiter / talent,
  hiring manager, interviewer, referrer, other). Pick someone you already have and **Link
  person**, or **New person** to add someone and link them in one go (they start at the
  application's company).
- **Log activity**: record a call, email, message or anything else on the timeline.
- **Timeline**: every stage move, activity, interview change and offer, in order.

### Waiting to hear back

When you've replied to someone and the ball is in their court, press **I've replied,
waiting** (on the application page, or on a person's page for conversations that aren't about
one application). It goes on the timeline, the application shows how long you've been
waiting, and it appears under *Waiting to hear back* on Today instead of *Gone quiet*.
Press **Heard back** when they reply; moving the application to another stage clears it too.

When they've replied but you haven't read it properly yet (it came in on your phone, or it's
long), press **They've replied** instead, whether or not you were waiting. It goes to the top
of Today, under *Your move* in *Replies to read*, until you press **Read it**; the applications list and board mark it *reply to read*. Saying
**I've replied, waiting** clears it too. A reply to read is never *gone quiet* or a follow-up to chase; moving the application to
another stage clears it, and undoing the move puts it back.

**How long people usually take.** Each time a wait ends because they got back to you, the
app remembers how long it took, in working days (weekends don't count: waiting from a Friday
and hearing back on the Monday is one working day). That's when you press **They've replied**
or **Heard back**, or an application you were waiting on moves to another stage (not one you
chose yourself: *Withdrawn*, *Declined*, *Accepted* or *Ghosted*); an application counts for
its recruiter. Chasing and marking them *Ghosted* don't count, and undoing a stage move takes
its reply back. Days are your local days. A person's tile, their page and their rows on Today
then say *Usually replies within 2 working days* (the middle of their reply times,
rounded up), or *Replied once, in 3 working days*; an application you're waiting on, on Today,
shows its recruiter's. When you've been waiting longer than that, Today and
their tile add *waiting 3 working days longer than usual*, which helps tell slow from gone.
Replies already in your applications' history (waiting, then *They replied* or *Heard back*,
or a move on) were counted when you updated; waits on people before then weren't recorded, so
theirs start now. Merging two people keeps both their replies, and deleting an application
keeps its recruiter's.

**Chasing people, or giving up on them.** For people (a recruiter you're talking to outside
any one application), three buttons sit under them in *Waiting to hear back* or *Replies to read* on
Today, and at the top of their page:

- **Chased**: you sent a nudge. You're waiting on them again from today, and
  Today says *Chased today* instead of how long you've been waiting.
- **Ghosted** and **Not interested**: you've stopped expecting
  anything. They leave Today; their page and their Recruiters tile say *Ghosted* or
  *Not interested*, with the date. Someone who ghosted you gets a ghost: in place of the
  person icon on their page, and before their name on their tile, on the applications they
  brought you (the list, the board, the quick view, the People card, company, agency and role
  pages), on calls, Today, Timeline entries and per-person rows, search results, and
  Insights (*Who you talk to* and the recruiter scorecard). Role lines say *(ghosted)* after
  the name.
  To take it off, whenever you like (a week later, say, when they were just slow), press
  **Not a ghost** on their page (or **Remove Not interested**), or the **×** on the chip on
  their tile; it says so, with **Undo**. Marking them as waiting or replied again takes it off
  too.

Each pops up a message with **Undo**, and goes on the Timeline. (For applications, **Ghosted**
is a stage: see [Today](#today-home).)

### Emails

Export an email from your mail client as a `.eml` file and drop it on the **Files** card.
The app reads its subject, sender, date and a snippet, and puts it on the timeline at the
time it was sent. The file itself is kept too.

### Archiving and deleting

- **Archive** (the switch in Details) hides an application from the list, board and Next
  actions without losing anything. It still counts in Insights, which are about your whole
  search. Find archived applications with the
  *Archived* filter on the Applications page, or with search. Switch it off to bring it back.
- **Delete** (top right of the page) removes it for good, with its interviews, offer,
  timeline, record of documents sent and links. Its notes and files stay (files become
  unattached), and so do calls about it (they're just no longer about an application).

Companies, agencies, roles and people have **Delete** too:

| Deleting | Also removes | Keeps |
|---|---|---|
| A company | its roles and their links | its people (no longer at a company), notes and files |
| A role | nothing else | — |
| An agency | nothing else | its recruiters and applications (no longer with an agency) |
| A person | them from applications and interviews, and their calls and meetings | the applications, and the roles they mentioned (no longer linked to them) |

A company or role that still has applications can't be deleted: delete or archive those
first. Your data repo's history still has everything until you rewrite it.

## People, recruiters and agencies

The **Recruiters** page lists everyone as small tiles, favourites first, then A to Z by name: agency recruiters,
people at companies and independents. Each tile has their job title, a chip for their agency
or company (click it to open that page), *Waiting* or *Reply to read* if that's where things
stand (or *Ghosted* / *Not interested*, with a **×** to take it off; someone who ghosted you
also gets a ghost before their name), and every email, phone number and link (click to email, call or open; long ones end in
"…", and hovering shows them in full). The pencil edits a person; open their page to delete
them.

- **Search** matches names, job titles, agencies, companies, emails and numbers.
- **All / Agencies / Companies / Independent / Favourites / Ghosted** narrows it to agency
  recruiters, people at companies, independents, your favourites, or everyone who ghosted you
  (with how many).
- **Agencies**: every agency as a chip with how many people and applications in progress it
  has, including ones with nobody in them yet.

**Favourites.** Star the people you rate (a recruiter who's genuinely good to work with): the
☆ on their tile, or next to their name on their page. A favourite gets a gold star next to
their name in the same places as the ghost (see *Ghosted* above): their tile and page, the
applications they brought you, calls, Today, the Timeline, search and Insights; role
lines put a ★ after the name. Favourites come first on the Recruiters page. Click the star
again to unstar them; editing a person never changes it.

**New agency** and **New person** are at the top right. In a person's form you can also type
a new agency or company name and pick **+ Add "…"** to create it there and then.

**One person per email address and LinkedIn profile.** Adding someone (or adding a detail to
someone) with an email address or LinkedIn profile another person already has is refused, and
the message says who has it. Emails match whatever their case; LinkedIn profiles match however
the link is written (with or without `https://`, `www.`, a trailing `/` or `?…` on the end),
and an email written as `mailto:…` counts as the address.
Phone numbers and other links can be shared. People who shared one before this rule can still
be edited: merge them.

Each **person** has a page, a dossier: their job title and where they work, how to reach
them, your calls and meetings with them, the roles they've mentioned, every application they're part of (and as what),
the interviews they were in, and your notes, links and files about them. Click a name anywhere to open it; **Timeline**,
**Edit**, **Merge…** and **Delete** are at the top. **Timeline** opens the
[Timeline](#timeline) filtered to them, over all time: calls, the roles they pitched, the
applications and interviews they're on, and notes about them or those roles.

**Two records of the same person?** On the one to keep, press **Merge…** and pick the other
(people with the same name, email address or LinkedIn profile are listed first). Everything
on the other record moves to this one: contact details it doesn't already have, calls, the
roles they pitched, the applications and interviews they're on, and notes, links, files and
to-dos about them. This person's own details win: the other record's job title is used if
this one has none, its agency or company if this one has neither, and its *waiting*, *reply
to read*, *ghosted* or *not interested* state if this one has none of those; the later of the
two *chased* dates stays, and the favourite star if either had it. Then the other record is deleted. When someone looks like
a duplicate, their page says so in a yellow note with a one-click **Merge into this one**, or
**Not the same person** to stop suggesting that pair (remembered in this browser).
Deleting a person instead takes their calls with them and leaves their roles, applications and
the rest without them, so for duplicates, merge.

### Calls and meetings

For a call or meeting with someone that isn't an interview round, such as a catch-up with a
recruiter about the market, or a chat with a hiring manager before you apply, use **Calls and
meetings** on their page:

- **Book a call**: who it's with, what it's about, when, how (phone, video or in person), a
  meeting link or where, and an agenda (what to ask, what to tell them).
- To log a call that already happened, pick **Happened** as its status and say how it went.
- Once its time has passed, it waits under *How did it go?* on Today. **Happened**
  asks for notes; **Didn't happen** asks what happened, then closes it.
- If it's about one application, pick it under **About an application**; the call then shows
  with that application on the Timeline.
- Under a call that happened, **Add roles from this call** adds the roles they mentioned, to
  [decide on](#roles-to-decide) later. The call lists them with their status.

Calls show under *Coming up* on Today, on the Timeline, and in
search. Interview rounds for an application stay on the application's page.

An **agency page** shows its recruiters and every role it put you forward for. Applications
for the same job through two routes (say, directly and via an agency) show a **duplicate
warning**.

## Companies

A list of companies with how many applications and how many need chasing. A **company
page** has its applications, roles (with whether you've applied, passed or still have to
decide, and **Add role**), people (**Add person** for the people you talk to there),
an *About* box, notes, links and files. **Edit** at the top renames it or sets its website,
and **Timeline** opens the Timeline filtered to it, over all time (its applications, roles,
the people there and notes); agencies have the same.

## Interviews

Every interview round across all applications: what's coming up, then everything earlier
or cancelled. On each row:

- **Reschedule** (a booked round, including one whose time has passed): pick the new time,
  choose **Who needed the change?** (*Me*, *Them*, *Both of us* or *Neither of us*) and, if you
  like, say why. Its end moves with it. The application's timeline, the
  [Timeline](#timeline) page and Today's recent activity say "Round 2 · … rescheduled", and under
  it "Moved from Tue 3 Nov, 10:00 to Thu 5 Nov, 14:00 · They needed the change · why" (with the
  years, if it moved into another year). Every move
  keeps its own entry, so a second reschedule doesn't overwrite the first. The same button is on
  the application's page, on Today, and in the quick
  view for its current round while the application is in an active stage.
- the pencil edits the round (everything on its form).

A round that's moved shows **Rescheduled** (or *Rescheduled ×2*), here and on the
application's page; hover it for the time it moved from. Changing a booked round's time from
its form counts too (the end moves with it there as well), but gives only the old and new time,
with nothing about who or why; changing its status in the same save doesn't count. Moves from
before a reschedule saved its times keep the words they already had ("… rescheduled", or
"… rescheduled: why" if you gave a reason), with no times or who, and those from before the
count came in have no old time on the badge. On the Timeline's lanes view, hovering a dot gives
a move's who and why in one sentence, without the times.

On the application's page, a round whose moves add up to a different day also says how far:
"Pushed back 3 days in total." It's the net, in calendar days where you are: pushed back five days
and then brought two forward says 3, one that ends up earlier says "Brought forward 2 days in
total.", and one back on its first day, or moved within a day, says nothing. Only moves with both
times saved count; when it shows a figure and some moves have none, it says how many it left out. [Insights](#insights) has the counts, under
*Rescheduling* on its Applications tab.

## Offers

Add an offer from the application's page. A **permanent** or **fixed-term** offer has base
salary, expected bonus, equity (in words, plus your estimate of its worth a year), employer
pension, holiday and benefits; a **contract** has a day rate, IR35 status and length. Add a
revised offer after negotiating: the newest one counts.

The **Offers** page puts them side by side, best first, with a *worth a year* figure:
salary + bonus + equity estimate + employer pension, or the day rate × 220 working days. It
compares like with like only roughly: read the rows, not just the total.

## Documents

Your CVs and cover letters, each with versions ("v3 (fintech)") and the file for each. The
application page records which version you sent, and the Documents page shows where each
version went. [Insights](#insights) compares how the versions do.

**CV files you've attached.** If you've been attaching your CV as a plain file (to an
application, to a role, or to a person, like the CV you sent a recruiter), the Documents page
finds them: PDF and Word files (`.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx`) named like a CV (*cv*, *resume*,
*résumé*, *curriculum*, e.g. `Resume_2026.pdf`) or where the very same file is attached in two
or more places (a role and its own application count as one place, so a job ad on both isn't
taken for a CV). Identical files are one entry (the app compares their contents, not their
names), saying where it's attached and which applications it counts for:

- a file on an application counts for that application (archived ones included);
- a file on a role counts for that role's applications, and one on a person for the
  applications they're on (as the recruiter, or on its People card), but only ones that have
  no CV recorded yet, so a CV you sent a recruiter last month doesn't claim the applications
  that went out with a newer one.

When every application a role or person led to already has a CV (or there are none yet, say a
CV you sent a recruiter before anything came of it), it shows *No applications without a CV
yet*. You can still make it a version; it then drops off the list, and comes back once there's
an application to record it on.

- **Make it a version**: add it to one of your CVs, or a new one, with a version name (it
  starts as the file name), and tick the applications it went out with (all of them to start
  with). The version gets its own copy of the file, and it's recorded as sent with the ones
  you ticked, on the day you applied (or, without an applied date, the day you attached it to
  that application, its role or its people). The files stay where you attached them too.
- **Record as sent**: when the file already is a version (you uploaded the same file as one),
  tick the applications that don't have it yet.

Applications you untick stay on offer here until they're recorded (record the CV they did get
on the application itself); **Not a CV** hides the whole file.
- **Not a CV**: hides it (remembered in this browser); **Show … you said aren't CVs** brings
  them back.

The card goes once there's nothing left to sort out, and the Insights CV tab says how many are
waiting.

## Notes

Markdown notes, attached to an application, company, agency or person, or on their own.
Each is a real `.md` file in your data folder (see [Your data](your-data.md#whats-in-the-folder)),
so you can also write and edit them in any editor. Tables, checklists and links work.

## Insights

How the search is going, for all time or the last 30 or 90 days. Four tabs: **The whole
picture**, **Applications**, **Recruiter conversations** and **CVs and cover letters**;
**Weekly activity** sits under all of them.

**The whole picture** (the tab Insights opens on) is everything in one Sankey diagram: every
role, from where it came from to where it is now.

- On the left, where it came from: **Through a recruiter** (someone pitched the role, or the
  application went through a recruiter or agency), **Referred** (a referral) or **You found
  it** (anything else).
- Then roles you passed on go to **Decided not to apply**, roles with no application yet to
  **Deciding whether to apply**, and applications through their stages from *Interested*.
- From each stage, a spur to what's still waiting there, just after the stage (*Deciding
  whether to apply* and *Decided not to apply* usually sit beside *Applied*). How things ended
  (Rejected, Withdrawn, Ghosted, Accepted…) can follow any stage, so it lines up on the right.

It counts roles and applications added in the date range; a role you applied to twice counts
twice. As in *Where applications go*, an application moved back counts at the furthest stage it
reached.

**Applications**

- **Where applications go**: a Sankey diagram of applications flowing through the stages,
  starting at *Interested*; filter by route, hover for counts. Each stage has a spur to where
  the applications still there are waiting: *Deciding whether to apply* (Interested, if your
  first stage comes before Applied), *Waiting to hear back* (Applied), *Still screening*, *Still
  interviewing*, *In final rounds*, *Deciding on the offer*, or *Still at …* for stages of your
  own. These sit just after their stage; how things ended lines up on the right. The diagram
  arranges each column so flows cross each other, or run through a stage, as little as it can.
  A label too long for the gap wraps; on a narrow screen the diagram scrolls sideways.
- **Stage by stage**: how many reached each stage, how many moved on, and the median time
  spent there.
- **Direct vs recruiter**: the share of each route's applications that got to each stage.
- **Recruiter scorecard**: per recruiter or agency, roles sent, how many got interviews,
  where they ended up, how long until the first update, and when you last heard from them.
- **Rescheduling**: how many booked rounds moved (a booked round has a time now, or has moved), and
  every move split by who needed the change: *Me*, *Them*, *Both of us*, *Neither of us* or *Not
  recorded*. A round that moves twice is one round and two moves. *Not recorded* holds moves
  made on the round's form, and older moves the app only has a count for, so they aren't put down
  to anyone. Companies and recruiters show only from 3 moved rounds, as plain counts, because fewer
  could look like a pattern; with none that far, there's no list. It counts what happened and
  doesn't say what moves caused. A move can have no date of its own, so the date range goes by when
  the application was added, whenever the move was: a move last week on an application added
  three months ago isn't in *Last 30 days*. A removed round isn't counted.

**Recruiter conversations**: what happens before an application. A *pitched role* is one with
a person on it (someone told you about it: **Add roles from this call**, or a person set on
the role); roles you found yourself aren't counted here.

- **The numbers**: calls held (and booked ahead), people you talked to or were pitched by,
  roles pitched, how many you applied for (and the apply rate: applied out of the roles
  you've decided on), passed on and are still deciding about (roles you're only *Interested*
  in count as still deciding; ones closed while only interested count as passed, but not
  towards the time to decide), interviews and offers that came
  from pitched roles, ghosted applications from pitches, and the median time to decide (from
  adding the role to applying or passing; a backdated application counts from its first
  stage).
- **Where pitched roles go**: a Sankey diagram from *Pitched to you* to *Applied*, *Decided
  not to apply* or *Deciding whether to apply*, and from *Applied* to *In progress*, *Offer*,
  *Closed* or *Ghosted* (a role's best application counts once). As in the other diagrams,
  *Deciding whether to apply* and *Decided not to apply* sit beside *Applied*, and where
  applications ended up on the right.
- **Who you talk to**: per person or per agency, calls held, the last call, roles pitched,
  applied, passed, deciding, the apply rate, interviews, and where the applications went. A
  person you've marked *Ghosted* or *Not interested* says so.
- **Why you passed**: the reasons you gave when passing on pitched roles, newest first.

Calls count in a date range by when they happen; roles by when you added them. A call
counts as held once it's marked *Happened*; one still marked booked after its time waits on
Today for you to say how it went.

**CVs and cover letters**: an A/B test of your CV versions. It works from what you record:
add a CV on [Documents](#documents) with a new version each time you change it, then on each
application pick the version you sent (its *Documents sent* card). Two applications got the
same CV when you picked the same version; the app doesn't read the files. CV files you
attached to applications as plain files can be made versions in one go: see
[CV files you've attached](#documents).

- **The table**: each CV, and under it each of its versions (when it has more than one),
  against the applications you sent added in the date range. *Sent* means it got to *Applied*
  (or past it, or was closed after it), or has a document recorded; one you closed before
  applying wasn't sent. In a custom workflow without an `applied` stage, *Applied* is the first
  active stage after the starting one. For each: how many you **sent**; how many **heard back**
  after applying (a move past *Applied*, a call, email or message logged, a rejection, an
  interview round or an offer; *Ghosted*, *Withdrawn* and *Declined* aren't replies, and nor
  is moving it to *Applied* yourself; logged contact has no direction, so a chaser you logged
  counts too); how many got a **screen or interview** (a stage past *Applied* that isn't
  closed, or an interview round that wasn't cancelled); **how sure** that rate is (the dot,
  with a bar for where it likely really is: a 95% range, wide when there are few
  applications); **offers** (an offer recorded, or the `offer` stage or a success stage);
  where they are now (open, ghosted, closed); and the **typical reply** time (the median from
  the day it got to *Applied* to the first reply).
  *No CV recorded* is a row of its own, so you can compare with and without. **CVs** /
  **Cover letters** switches between them; the route filter compares like with like.
- **Compare**: pick two (versions, whole documents or *No CV recorded*) and a measure
  (*Heard back*, *Screen or interview* or *Offer*). It says in words whether the gap is
  **likely real**, **a hint** (worth watching), **could be chance**, or there are **too few to
  tell** (fewer than five sent on either side), using Fisher's exact test, which holds up with
  small numbers. It starts on your two most-sent versions. A CV can't be compared with one of
  its own versions (they share applications); two versions sent with the same application
  share it too, so record the one you actually sent.

- **Weekly activity**: applications added and sent, stage moves, interviews, calls held and
  roles pitched per week.

## Search

Type in the box at the top (or press <kbd>/</kbd>) and press Enter. It finds applications,
companies, agencies, people (including their emails and phone numbers), interview prep and
debriefs, calls and meetings (their agenda and notes), to-dos, offers, timeline entries, notes,
documents, files, emails and links. Every word
must match; case and accents don't matter.

## Picking dates

Every calendar rings today in indigo, so you can see where you are. Weeks start on Monday, and
dates read day, month, year ("31 Oct 2026", "30 Oct 2026, 19:25"), whatever language your
browser is set to.

## Language

The language button at the top right (beside light/dark mode, showing a globe and your current
language's name) lists the languages the app
comes in, each by its own name. For now that's English, with more *Coming soon!*. Your choice
is kept in this browser; until you pick one, the app follows your browser's language if it
has it, and English otherwise. What you've typed (companies, notes, stage names you've renamed
or added) is never translated.

Under the languages, *Don't see your language?* opens [a guide to adding one](adding-a-language.md)
on GitHub, in a new tab. It's an ordinary link: the app itself never fetches anything from there.

The moon/sun button at the top right.
